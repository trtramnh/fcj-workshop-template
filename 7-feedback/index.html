Sharing and Feedback

General Assessment

1. Working Environment:

The working environment at FCJ is highly professional, friendly, and open. From the beginning of the program, I felt welcomed and comfortable communicating with mentors, administrators, and other members. Everyone is encouraged to share ideas, ask questions, and discuss difficulties without feeling pressured or afraid of making mistakes.

Whenever I encounter a technical issue or have difficulty understanding a concept, the mentors are always willing to listen and provide support. They also proactively check on the progress of participants and make sure that no one is left behind. This creates a positive learning atmosphere in which I feel motivated to improve myself and become more responsible for my assigned tasks.

The office space is also clean, organized, and comfortable. A tidy working environment helps me maintain concentration and work more efficiently. Overall, the combination of a professional atmosphere and supportive people has made my experience at FCJ both productive and enjoyable.

2. Support from Mentors and the Admin Team:

The mentors provide enthusiastic, detailed, and practical guidance throughout the program. When I do not fully understand a technical concept, they explain it clearly and often provide examples so that I can understand how the knowledge is applied in a real project. Instead of simply giving the final answer, the mentors encourage me to research the problem, test possible solutions, and develop my own way of thinking.

I especially appreciate that the mentors always encourage participants to ask questions and explore topics in greater depth. This approach has helped me become more confident when discussing technical problems and more independent when searching for information. Through their guidance, I have gradually improved my problem-solving ability and learned how to approach complex tasks step by step.

The admin team also provides valuable support by regularly communicating important announcements, schedules, deadlines, and program requirements. They make sure that participants receive the necessary documents and learning resources in a timely manner. Their careful coordination creates favorable conditions for everyone to focus on learning and completing their work effectively.

3. Relevance to My Academic Major:

The work assigned to me and my team is highly relevant to my academic major and future career direction. The program gives me an opportunity to apply the knowledge I have learned at university to practical situations instead of only studying concepts in theory.

Through the assigned workshops and project activities, I have gained greater exposure to cloud computing and learned more about how applications can be designed, deployed, and operated in a cloud environment. This experience has helped me understand that software development involves more than writing code. It also requires knowledge of system architecture, security, data management, performance, monitoring, and scalability.

In addition to technical knowledge, I have developed several important professional skills. I learned how to collaborate with team members, divide responsibilities, communicate progress, and discuss technical decisions. I also became more aware of the importance of listening to different opinions and presenting my ideas clearly and respectfully.

These experiences have complemented my academic studies and provided me with a clearer understanding of the skills required in the technology industry. They have also helped me identify the areas that I would like to continue studying, particularly cloud computing, AWS services, and artificial intelligence.

4. Company Culture and Team Spirit:

The culture at FCJ is positive, respectful, and collaborative. Everyone takes responsibility for their assigned work while remaining willing to support others when difficulties arise. Although each member may be responsible for different tasks, the overall progress of the team is considered a shared responsibility.

During teamwork activities, members are encouraged to exchange ideas, provide feedback, and discuss different approaches. When a problem occurs, the team focuses on finding a solution rather than blaming individuals. This creates a safe environment where everyone can learn from mistakes and continuously improve.

The team spirit has also taught me that effective collaboration requires more than simply completing individual tasks. It requires regular communication, clear task allocation, active listening, and timely updates. Through this experience, I have become more responsible for my work and more aware of how my contribution can affect the progress of the whole team.

Even though I participated as a program member, I felt that my opinions and efforts were respected. This sense of belonging made me more motivated to contribute actively and complete my responsibilities to the best of my ability.

5. Internship Policies and Benefits:

One of the most valuable benefits of joining the program is the opportunity to participate in professional events and learn directly from industry experts and experienced professionals. These activities allow me to expand my knowledge beyond the regular workshops and project tasks.

I was fortunate to have the opportunity to attend the Agent Forge – Deepdive Day 1 event at Bitexco Financial Tower, Ho Chi Minh City, on August 1, 2026. This was a truly valuable experience because I was able to listen to professional sharing sessions, learn more about current developments in cloud computing and artificial intelligence, and gain additional perspectives from people with practical experience in the industry.

The event also gave me an opportunity to learn more about Amazon Bedrock AgentCore and the development of AI agents on AWS. Although some of the topics were advanced, they helped me better understand the potential applications of agentic AI and motivated me to continue researching this area in the future.

In addition to gaining technical knowledge, participating in the event allowed me to connect with other learners, professionals, and members of the technology community. These interactions helped me become more confident in professional communication and showed me the importance of building relationships within the industry.

Overall, the opportunities provided by the program have contributed significantly to both my technical and personal development. They have allowed me to learn not only from documents and project tasks but also from real people, professional events, and practical experiences.

Other Questions

1. What Satisfied You Most During Your Internship?

What satisfied me most was the opportunity to design and deploy a real-world Cloud-Native application like Snaptics (AI-powered personal expense management and receipt scanning platform). I got hands-on experience working directly with core AWS services such as Amazon S3, SQS, ECS Fargate, AWS Amplify, CloudFront, CloudWatch, as well as AI/OCR integrations. Practical engineering experience and guidance from mentors were invaluable.

2. What Do You Think the Company Should Improve for Future Interns?

It would be great if the program could host additional technical workshops or deep-dive sharing sessions focused on advanced AWS and AI topics (such as Serverless Architecture, Event-Driven Design, Amazon Bedrock AgentCore, or Cloud Cost Optimization) to further challenge future interns.

3. If Recommending to a Friend, Would You Suggest They Intern Here? Why?

I would definitely recommend it. This environment provides a perfect combination of practical technical experience, the opportunity to build a complete cloud product like Snaptics, an enthusiastic and supportive learning environment, and a solid foundation in Cloud Computing & AI on AWS.

Suggestions & Expectations

1. Do You Have Any Suggestions to Improve the Internship Experience?

I hope the program can create more opportunities for interns to work directly at the office. Having regular offline work sessions would help interns easily discuss tasks with mentors, receive faster feedback, collaborate better with team members, and gain a clearer understanding of the actual corporate working environment.

In addition, the program could establish an office work schedule combined with workshops, progress reviews, or technical sharing sessions. This would help interns not only enhance their technical knowledge but also practice communication skills, professional work ethics, and networking with everyone in the company.

2. Would You Like to Continue This Program in the Future?

Yes, I really hope to have the opportunity to continue accompanying the program, especially participating in internships and working directly at the office more often. I want to continue developing the Snaptics application, consolidate my AWS knowledge, and dive deeper into Cloud, Serverless, and Agentic AI solutions.

3. Other Comments:

I would like to express my sincere thanks to the FCAJ team, mentors, and the admin team for always being dedicated and enthusiastically supportive throughout the program. The practical knowledge, opportunities to attend professional events, and office work experiences have helped me learn many valuable things. I hope future programs will continue to expand in-person office activities so that interns have the chance to experience the corporate environment in a more complete and realistic way.
